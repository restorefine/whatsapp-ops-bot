=====================================================================
                    RESTOREFINE OPS BOT - HOW TO USE IT
                        A guide for the two founders
=====================================================================


1. WHAT IS IT?
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The Ops Bot is a WhatsApp contact that tells you what is happening in
ClickUp: what's due, what's late, who is working on what, and which
social media posts are going out.

You don't need to open ClickUp. Just send the bot a short command on
WhatsApp and it replies within a few seconds.

  - The bot lives on our BUSINESS WhatsApp number.
  - You message it from your own PERSONAL WhatsApp, like any other chat.
  - Tip: save the business number in your contacts as "Ops Bot" and pin
    the chat so it's always at the top.


2. WHO CAN USE IT?
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Only the two founders. The bot knows your two personal numbers and
replies to nobody else.

  - If anyone else messages the business number, the bot ignores them
    completely. They get no reply and see none of our information.
  - The bot never replies to team members. It only messages them when
    a founder sends /remind (see below). It does quietly note WHEN
    they last messaged the business number, so it knows whether it
    can message them for free.
  - The bot only reads ClickUp. It never changes, creates or deletes
    anything there.


3. THE COMMANDS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Type any of these into the chat and send it.

  /due      What's due TODAY and TOMORROW
            Lists every unfinished task and every social media post
            due today and tomorrow, with who is on each one. It ends
            with a count of older tasks that are still overdue.
            (The automatic 08:00 version is paused for now. See section 5.)
            (/today does the same thing.)

  /update   The full monthly overview
            - Headline numbers: due this month, completed, still open,
              overdue
            - A team table: how many tasks each person has open, how
              many are late, how many they finished this month
            - Overdue work left over from earlier months
            - A day-by-day calendar of this month
                ✅ = done   ⏳ = still open   ⚠️ = overdue
            - A summary of social media uploads
            - A preview of next month

  /uploads  The social media posting calendar for this month
            One post per line, with how many have been posted, how
            many are coming up and how many were missed. It also lists
            posts from the last 30 days that were never marked as
            posted.

  /uploads next
            The posting calendar for NEXT month.

  /<name>   One person's work, for example:  /sunil, /arpan, /himal, /sagun, /sonam
            Shows their numbers, then their overdue tasks, their open
            tasks grouped by status, and what they finished this month.

  /remind <name>
            Sends that person their tasks due TODAY, for example:
            /remind sunil
            The message ends with "Reply OK to confirm you've seen
            this". If they have nothing due today, nothing is sent.
            It never costs money. See section 6 for how.

  /team     Everyone in the ClickUp workspace, with the exact command
            to type for each person. Use this if you're not sure how
            to spell someone's name.

  /help     Shows the list of commands.


4. HOW TO TYPE COMMANDS (it's forgiving)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  - The slash is optional:          "due" works the same as "/due"
  - Capitals don't matter:          "/UPDATE" = "/update"
  - Punctuation at the end is fine: "/due?" or "/due." still work
  - Names can be shortened:         "/su" finds Sunil
  - Full names work too:            "/sunil paudel"
  - If a name matches more than one person, the bot lists them and
    you pick the right one.
  - If the bot doesn't understand you, it replies with the list of
    commands.
  - Text only. Photos, voice notes and stickers get the reply
    "I only understand text commands".


5. THE DAILY 08:00 REMINDER  (PAUSED FOR NOW)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  ** The automatic morning message is switched off for now. Send /due
     whenever you want the list. The rest of this section describes
     how it works if it's switched back on. **

Every morning at 08:00, without you asking, the bot sends both
founders the "Due soon" message. It's the same as /due:

  📌 Due today         - tasks that need finishing today
  📤 Posting today     - social media posts going out today
  🔜 Due tomorrow      - tasks due tomorrow
  📤 Posting tomorrow  - social media posts going out tomorrow
  ⚠️ Older overdue     - how many older tasks are still late

So every task is flagged TWICE: the morning before it's due, and on
the morning it's due. For example, a task due on Friday shows up in
Thursday's message under "Due tomorrow" and in Friday's message under
"Due today".

Finished tasks and posts are left out. If nothing is due, the message
says "Nothing due today or tomorrow. 🎉"

Sometimes the morning message is SHORTER and only shows the numbers,
e.g. "3 items are due today and 2 due tomorrow. Reply /due to see the
full list." Section 6 explains why. Just reply /due and the full list
arrives.


6. IS IT FREE? (the WhatsApp 24-hour rule)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Short answer: using the bot is free. The morning reminder is free too,
as long as you've messaged the bot in the last 24 hours.

How WhatsApp charges businesses:

  FREE - Anything YOU send to the bot.
         Incoming messages never cost anything.

  FREE - The bot's replies to your commands.
         When you message the bot, a free 24-hour window opens for
         you. Anything the bot sends you in that window costs nothing.

  FREE - The 08:00 reminder, IF you've messaged the bot in the
         previous 24 hours (your window is still open).

  SMALL CHARGE - The 08:00 reminder, IF you HAVEN'T messaged the bot
         in the previous 24 hours. WhatsApp then makes the bot send a
         short pre-approved message (the "template") with just the
         numbers. That costs a few pence. Reply /due and you get the
         full list for free, because your reply opens a new window.

  What this means day to day:
    - Messaged the bot yesterday? You get the full list at 08:00.
      Free.
    - Haven't messaged it for a while? You get the short version at
      08:00 (a few pence). Reply /due for the full list. Free.

/remind is ALWAYS free:

  - If the person has messaged the business number in the last 24
    hours (including normal chats with you in the WhatsApp Business
    app), the bot sends the reminder itself.
    You see: "✅ Sent Sunil a reminder: 3 tasks due today."
  - If they haven't, the bot does NOT message them. It sends YOU a
    link instead. Tap it, WhatsApp opens their chat with the reminder
    already typed, and you press send.
    Tip: send it from the WhatsApp Business app. When they reply "OK",
    their 24 hours start, and the next /remind goes out by itself.

Nothing else costs money on the WhatsApp side. Your normal phone data
applies, as with any WhatsApp chat.


7. TIPS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  ★ Keep it free: send the bot one command each day, e.g. /due in the
    afternoon. That keeps your window open, so the next morning's full
    reminder is free.

  ★ Morning routine: send /due, then send /<name> for
    anyone who has a lot due or a lot overdue.

  ★ Weekly check: send /update once a week (e.g. Monday morning) to
    see the whole month and how the team is doing.

  ★ Social media: send /uploads to check posts are going out, and
    /uploads next near the end of the month to check next month is
    planned.

  ★ Forgot a name? Send /team and copy the command shown.

  ★ Pin the chat and save the number as "Ops Bot".


8. THE BOT IS ONLY AS GOOD AS CLICKUP
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The bot reads ClickUp live every time you ask, so what it shows
depends on how ClickUp is kept. Please make sure the team:

  - Gives every task a DUE DATE. Tasks with no due date never appear
    in the reminder or the calendar. They only appear in /<name> and
    in the team table.
  - ASSIGNS every task to someone. Otherwise it shows as
    "Unassigned".
  - Marks tasks as COMPLETE when finished. Otherwise they count as
    open or overdue.
  - Puts social media posts in the "Uploads" folder in ClickUp, with
    the posting date as the due date, and marks them complete once
    posted.

What the words mean:
  Done / Posted - the task is marked complete in ClickUp
  Open          - not complete yet
  Overdue / Late / Missed
                - the due date has passed and it isn't complete

Changes made in ClickUp can take up to a minute to show in the bot.


9. GOOD TO KNOW
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Long replies (like /update) can arrive as several messages in a
    row. That's normal.
  - Long lists are cut short with "+5 more" (or similar) to keep them
    readable. Send /<name> to see one person's full list.
  - Numbers and the team table appear in a fixed-width font so the
    columns line up. Turn your phone sideways if they look squashed.
  - Every report shows the date and time at the top, so you know it's
    current.
  - The bot doesn't store any data. It fetches from ClickUp fresh
    every time.


10. IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  No reply at all
    -> Make sure you're messaging the BUSINESS number from your own
       personal WhatsApp number.
    -> Wait a minute and try again. If there's still nothing, tell
       whoever looks after the bot. The server may be down.

  "Couldn't fetch data from ClickUp right now"
    -> ClickUp is busy or briefly unavailable. Try again in a minute.

  "I don't recognise /..."
    -> The name wasn't found. Send /team to see the right commands.

  Didn't get the 08:00 message
    -> If the server was restarting or down at exactly 08:00, that
       day's reminder is skipped. Send /due to get it now.

  A task is missing from the list
    -> Check it in ClickUp: does it have a due date, and is it still
       open (not marked complete)?
